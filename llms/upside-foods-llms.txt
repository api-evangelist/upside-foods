# UPSIDE Foods

> UPSIDE Foods is a cultivated meat company that grows real chicken directly from animal cells — no factory farming — in cultivator tanks, starting from a master cell bank derived from a single heritage-breed chicken egg. The company supplies cultivated chicken to foodservice partners and does not publish a public API or developer program.

UPSIDE Foods is a consumer food company backed by SoftBank Vision Fund and Threshold Ventures. As of 2026-07-21 it publishes no developer portal, API documentation, machine-readable specs, or /.well-known/ discovery surface.
